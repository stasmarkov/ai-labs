# Ship report: SL-39 duplicate photos and best-photo pick

## Summary

SL-39 adds a seeded generator of augmented photo copies (`photos:make-duplicates`), clustering by image vector (`photos:duplicates`), a best-photo judgement per group by the photo-vision model (`--judge`), a review page at `/ai/photos/duplicates` (Keep / Undo / Pick another, marks only), an eval written to `run.json` and `BENCHMARKS.md`, and the guide at `/ai/guides/photo-duplicates`.

All SL-39 work is still uncommitted on `master`.

## Confirmed review findings

### Major

- `app/Console/Commands/PhotoDuplicatesCommand.php:69`: `--judge` re-queues reviewed groups, and `queue()` wipes their completed judgement. Fix: skip groups that have a review, or that already have a completed judgement for the same provider, model and prompt (this is gap task 12).
- `app/Jobs/JudgeDuplicateGroupJob.php:26`: `$timeout = 600` is above the database queue `retry_after = 480`, so a slow job is reserved twice and `abandon()` races the running job. Fix: lower `$timeout` below `retry_after`, or raise `DB_QUEUE_RETRY_AFTER` above 600.
- `app/Services/PhotoDuplicateGeneratorService.php:91`: `--fresh` cascades leave orphaned `review_status` marks, groups stuck as reviewed, and completed judgements with `best_photo_id` NULL. Fix: clear the members' `review_status` and delete the affected groups before deleting augmented rows.
- `app/Repositories/EloquentDuplicateGroupRepository.php:35`: every rebuild deletes and recreates unreviewed groups, so all completed judgements cascade away and are re-judged. Fix: keep groups whose sorted member set is unchanged, or key judgements by a member-set hash.
- `app/Repositories/EloquentDuplicateReviewRepository.php:98`: `listing_photos.review_status` is global, but groups and reviews are per label, so marks drift and leak across labels. Fix: derive the status from `photo_duplicate_reviews` plus membership, or scope marks per group.
- `config/ai.php:132`: the cutoff default is 0.92 in config, 0.98 in the config comment and 0.98 in two service fallbacks. The stored `photo_duplicate_groups.cutoff` is never read. Fix: read the cutoff from one place, and show the stored per-group cutoff or drop the column.

### Minor

- `app/Services/DuplicateReviewService.php:65`: keep and undo accept groups of any vector label. Fix: scope `memberIds`/`find` to the current label and return 404 otherwise.
- `app/Services/DuplicateClusterService.php:37`: `reviewedPhotoIds($label)` ignores old-label marks, so they show up in new-label cards. Fix: scope marks per label or group (same root cause as the major finding above).
- `app/Services/DuplicateReviewService.php:40`: the page shows the configured cutoff, not the cutoff the groups were built with. Fix: read `photo_duplicate_groups.cutoff`.
- `database/migrations/2026_11_12_130000_add_duplicate_columns_to_listing_photos_table.php:17`: the `duplicate_of` cascade on a Stanford `--fresh` re-import orphans the augmented JPEGs. Fix: restrict the delete, or collect the dependent augmented paths in `PhotoImportService`.
- `app/Ai/PhotoBestPickAgent.php:26`: `MAX_PHOTOS = 6` is not tied to `ai.photo_duplicates.max_group`. Fix: clamp the cap to `PhotoBestPickAgent::MAX_PHOTOS`, or derive one from the other.
- `resources/guides/photo-duplicates.php:41`: the guide calls mirrored copies the hard case, but the data shows cropped is (recall 0.75 at 0.92; mirrored is 1.00). Fix: rewrite the failures paragraph around cropped copies.
- `app/Http/Controllers/KeepDuplicatePhotoController.php:14`: an overlong numeric `{group}` causes a TypeError and a 500. Fix: constrain the route with `->where('group', '[1-9][0-9]{0,17}')`.
- `database/migrations/2026_11_12_160000_create_photo_duplicate_reviews_table.php:19`: `kept_photo_id` and `best_photo_id` (in `2026_11_12_150000`) have no index. Fix: add `->index()` to both.
- `app/DTOs/DuplicateJudgeTarget.php:13`: `judgementId`, `groupId` and `promptVersion` are never read. Fix: remove them, or use `promptVersion` in the provider-changed check.
